Privacy Policy for Serpent Run

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Serpent Run (Android package ID: com.muneeb.neonserpents) is an arcade survival game published by Muneeb. This Privacy Policy describes what data the application stores locally, what third-party data Google AdMob processes for advertising, and how you can control or delete your data at any time.

1. No User Account & No Sale of Personal Data

  • No Account Required: Serpent Run does not require registration, login credentials, names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Developer Backend Servers: The developer does not operate remote analytics, tracking, or telemetry servers.
  • No Personal Data Sold: We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

2. Local On-Device Storage & How to Delete Your Data

To save your offline gameplay progress and preferences, Serpent Run stores the following keys exclusively on your device inside WebView/browser localStorage:

  • serpentRun_save_v2 — Player level, XP, coins, career statistics, daily/weekly/elite mission progress, claimed achievements, and audio/control settings.
  • serpentRun_unlockedSkins & serpentRun_selectedSkin — Unlocked and equipped snake skins.
  • neonSerpents_bestTime_easy|medium|hard & neonSerpents_bestLength_easy|medium|hard — High scores per difficulty mode.
  • serpentRun_graphicsQuality, serpentRun_fpsCounter, serpentRun_lbCollapsed, neonSerpents_muted — Display, HUD, and mute preferences.
  • serpentRun_adConsent_v1 — Your saved Google UMP / Ad personalization consent state.

Android cloud auto-backup and device-to-device extraction are disabled (android:allowBackup="false"), so this data stays strictly on your device.

Deleting Your Data: You can permanently erase all locally stored data at any time inside the game by opening Settings → Delete My Data and confirming deletion, or by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling the app in Android system settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Android Advertising ID

The Android version of Serpent Run integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display optional rewarded video advertisements (Revive Snake, 2x Match Coins, Bonus Chest, 1-Match Skin Trial), frequency-capped interstitial advertisements between completed matches, and menu-only banner advertisements.

When online and permitted by your consent settings, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and transmit the following categories of data to Google over encrypted HTTPS connections:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and app set ID, used for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, attribution, and (only when consented) personalized advertising.
  • Approximate (Coarse) Location & Network Info: Device IP address and network connection type, used by Google to estimate general geographic region for regional privacy compliance and relevant ad delivery.
  • App Interactions & Ad Diagnostics: Ad impressions, ad clicks, reward completion callbacks, and SDK crash/performance diagnostics related to ad loading.

Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google Partner Sites policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

4. User Consent (UMP), Personalized Ads & Opt-Out Controls

Before requesting advertisements, Serpent Run checks regional consent requirements using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) (including GDPR in the EEA/UK and applicable US state privacy laws):

  • No Personalized Ads Before Consent: Personalized ads are never requested before required consent is obtained. By default, or whenever consent is not granted, ad requests are restricted to Non-Personalized Ads (npa: true).
  • In-App Consent Management: You can review or change your ad personalization choice at any time in Settings → Ad Consent Preferences.
  • Device-Level Advertising ID Controls: On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time under Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Device Permissions

Serpent Run requests only the minimum Android permissions required for core gameplay and ad delivery:

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to check connectivity and load advertisements. Core gameplay works 100% offline.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ as described in Section 3.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — Used for optional in-game haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings → Vibration / Haptics).

Serpent Run never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos/media files, or precise GPS location.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Ad requests are configured with a general audience content rating guard and follow the target audience and family policy settings configured in Google Play Console and AdMob.
