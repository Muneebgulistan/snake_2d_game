Privacy Policy for Serpent Run

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Serpent Run (package ID: com.muneeb.neonserpents) is an arcade survival game published by Muneeb. This Privacy Policy explains how local game data, advertising, and user privacy are handled.

1. Local Game Data & Save Storage

Serpent Run does not require user registration or an account. Gameplay progress—including best survival times, highest snake lengths, player level, XP, coins, unlocked cosmetic skins, daily missions, achievements, and audio/control settings—is stored locally on your device using browser/WebView local storage (localStorage).

This local save data remains on your device and is not transmitted to developer-operated servers. You can reset your progress at any time via Settings → Reset Progress, or by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling the application.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android and supported versions of Serpent Run may integrate Google AdMob to display optional rewarded advertisements (such as reviving a snake, doubling match coins, or previewing a skin), frequency-capped interstitial advertisements between completed matches, and non-intrusive banner advertisements on menu subscreens.

When Google AdMob is active in a production release, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information in accordance with Google's privacy policies, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) or similar resettable advertising identifiers.
  • Coarse Location & Network Info: IP address (used to estimate general/coarse geographic region for relevant ad serving and fraud prevention).
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, clicks, reward completion events, and crash/performance diagnostics related to ad delivery.

For full details on how Google collects and processes data, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. User Consent, Personalized Ads & Opt-Out

Where required by applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area/UK or US state privacy regulations), users are presented with a consent choice via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or device-level privacy controls before personalized advertisements are served.

You can also reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

4. Device Permissions

Serpent Run requests only standard permissions necessary for core functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Used only to load advertisements when online. The core game remains playable offline.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (when AdMob SDK is enabled) — Used by Google Mobile Ads for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and ad attribution.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. When advertisements are served via Google AdMob, ad requests respect applicable age-appropriate and family policy settings configured in Google Play Console and AdMob.

6. Changes & Contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features, monetization options, or regulatory requirements are introduced. For questions regarding privacy or data practices, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the official Serpent Run Google Play Store page.
