Privacy Policy for Serpent Run

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Serpent Run is an offline game published by Muneeb. This policy explains what the app stores and how it is handled.

Information and storage

The game does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information. It saves your best survival time, best length, and sound preference on your device using local browser storage. This information stays on your device, is not sent to the developer, and is not shared with third parties. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes these records.

Permissions, advertising, and analytics

The game does not request sensitive device permissions and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the Serpent Run Google Play listing.
